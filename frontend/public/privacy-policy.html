Official Institutional Policy

Privacy Policy — Nandha LeetCode Intelligence

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 | Last Updated: November 17, 2026
Developer / Institution: Nandha Engineering College (Autonomous), Erode, Tamil Nadu, India

1. Overview

Nandha LeetCode Intelligence is an educational and institutional performance-tracking application developed specifically for students, faculty mentors, department heads (HODs), and administrators of Nandha Engineering College (Autonomous). The application monitors students' LeetCode weekly contest performance, attendance, problem-solving growth, and departmental programming analytics.

2. Information We Collect

A. Academic & Profile Information

  • Student Full Name and College Registration Number (Roll Number)
  • Academic Department (e.g. CSE, Cyber Security, IoT, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical)
  • Academic Year / Cohort Batch (I, II, III, IV Year)
  • Institutional Email Address
  • Public LeetCode Username

B. Public Coding Contest Data

  • Contest participation status, contest global ranking, and contest rating from public GraphQL endpoints.
  • Number of problems solved during designated weekly contests.

C. Device & Technical Identifiers

  • Push Notification Tokens (FCM): Used exclusively to deliver contest reminders and rank milestone notifications.
  • Hashed IP & Session Logs: One-way salted hashes used strictly for brute-force prevention and session security.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data collected is used exclusively for institutional academic tracking, calculating contest attendance, generating departmental performance analytics, and enabling faculty mentors to provide academic guidance.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade student data. Data is processed through trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For push notifications.
  • Institutional SMTP / Brevo: For automated contest report summaries.

5. Data Security

All network communications use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Cleartext HTTP communication is strictly blocked.

6. Account & Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your profile and contest tracking history, contact your Department HOD or email support@nandhaengg.org. Validated deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

7. Disclaimer Regarding LeetCode

Nandha LeetCode Intelligence is an independent institutional tracking tool developed by Nandha Engineering College. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by LeetCode or LeetCode LLC.
